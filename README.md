# Side B — support

Side B turns your music into retro machines: a pocket cassette player, a portable CD player, a suitcase record player, a reel-to-reel deck, a jukebox and a hi-fi stack with a working graphic equaliser. On a foldable iPhone, close the phone and the cover screen becomes the player; open it to flip through your albums.

## Getting started

- **Demo tape:** a tape of four synthesised songs is on the shelf from the first launch, so you can try everything straight away.
- **Apple Music:** tap **+ → From Apple Music**, allow access, and pick a playlist or album. It is wound onto a tape (up to 120 minutes). Playback needs an Apple Music subscription.
- **Your own files:** tap **+ → From your files** and pick MP3, M4A, WAV or AIFF files to make a mixtape.
- **Switch gadgets:** tap the gadget button on any screen, or go to Settings → Gadget.

## Common questions

**The tape doesn't start when I close the phone.** Check Settings → Folding → *Play when you close the phone*. On iPhones that don't fold, press Play.

**The equaliser doesn't change Apple Music.** Apple Music plays outside the app's audio, so the graphic equaliser, tape hiss effects on the music and wow & flutter apply to your own files and the demo tape.

**An Apple Music tape won't play.** Make sure you're signed in to Apple Music with an active subscription, and that Side B has Media & Apple Music access in the Settings app.
