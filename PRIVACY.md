# Side B — privacy policy

_Last updated: 1 October 2026_

Side B does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- **No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.** The app contains no third-party SDKs.
- **Your music stays on your device.** Tapes you make, the files you import and your settings are stored only on your iPhone or iPad.
- **Apple Music.** If you allow access, the app reads your Apple Music library and catalog through Apple's MusicKit to make tapes and play them. This is handled by Apple; Side B does not send it anywhere.
- **Apple Intelligence.** Mixtape title ideas are generated on your device with Apple's on-device model. Nothing leaves your device.
- **Motion sensors.** On foldable iPhones the app reads the device's orientation to tell how the phone is standing (for example, tent or tabletop). This stays on the device and is never stored.
